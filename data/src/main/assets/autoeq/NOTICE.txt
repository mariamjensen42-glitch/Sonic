Catalog format (tab separated, gzip):
  id, form factor, base64(brand), base64(model), preamp gain dB, filter chain

Filter chain entries are separated by ';' and each reads type,freq, gain dB, Q:
  L = low shelf, P = peaking, H = high shelf
